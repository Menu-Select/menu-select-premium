The problem

It's 3pm. You're at work, the afternoon is full, and nobody has decided what's for dinner. By 5, the easy answer is takeout again, not because you don't want to cook, but because deciding is the hard part.

Menu Select takes the deciding off your plate.

How it works

  1. Pick a dinner. Browse a curated set of weeknight dinners from your phone, whenever you have a minute.
  2. Get the groceries. Menu Select builds the shopping list. Remove what you already have, then send it to Instacart.
  3. Choose your recipe. Every dish links to recipes from cooks around the web, so you can pick the version that looks best to you.
  4. Cook tonight. Or plan the whole week at once and get one combined list for every dinner.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to cook at home more and decide less: individuals, couples and busy families, especially on weeknights when time and energy run short.

Who's behind it

I'm Steve, a parent and the founder of Menu Select. Running systems is my day job, and for years I've run our family dinners the same way: a planned rotation, a stocked pantry and a short weekly shop. Most of our dinners now come from Menu Select. I built it so other families could get dinner on the table without the daily scramble.
