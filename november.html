November dinners

Thirty dinners from Nov 1 to Dec 5, with no seafood. Swap a dinner you don't love or skip a night, and the shopping list follows.

0 Things to buy this month
0 Fresh items bought for one dinner

Swaps come from the same night first, ranked by how little they add to your shopping. When fewer than three fit, they borrow from other nights. Chicken and beef never land on back-to-back nights. Pork gets one night in weeks 1, 3 and 5, turkey one night in weeks 2 and 4, and nothing on the menu is seafood. Fridays stay a night off unless you swap in a dinner. A skipped night takes its groceries off the list and keeps its place, so you can bring it back. Dinners you swap out aren't suggested again.
