Menu Select

Privacy & Terms

Last updated October 19, 2026

Menu Select helps families pick dinners for the week and build a grocery list. This page explains what happens to your information when you use app.menu-selection.com, and the terms for using it.

What we store

You don't need an account to use Menu Select. Your favorites, weekly plan, pantry and freezer lists are saved only in your own web browser on your device. We don't receive or store them on our servers.

Because of this, your plan won't appear on another device, and clearing your browser's data will erase it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers.
  • We don't collect your name, address or payment details in the app.

Services we use

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.
  • Fonts and icons. Pages load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and public code libraries, which may see your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • Recipe photos and links. Photos load from Unsplash, and "Full recipe" links open the original recipe on other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Shopping with Instacart

When you choose to shop with Instacart, you leave Menu Select and go to Instacart's website or app. Menu Select only passes along the names of the grocery items on your list. Your Instacart account, orders, payments, prices, fees and delivery are handled by Instacart under its own privacy policy and terms. Menu Select is not owned by or affiliated with Instacart.

Recipes that call for wine or spirits mark those ingredients as optional, and Menu Select doesn't send them to Instacart.

Email summaries

"Email" buttons open your own email app with your list filled in. Menu Select doesn't send the email or see who you send it to.

Fees

The Menu Select app is free to use. Grocery prices, delivery fees, service fees and tips are set by Instacart and shown to you before you check out. The monthly Menu Select plan on Substack is a separate, optional paid subscription.

Not medical or nutrition advice

Menu Select offers dinner ideas and grocery lists for everyday family cooking. It is not medical, nutrition or dietary advice. If you are pregnant, have allergies, or have health or dietary needs, check ingredients carefully and follow the guidance of your doctor or other qualified professional.

Using Menu Select

  • Menu Select is intended for adults 18 and older.
  • Recipes, cook times and serving sizes are general guides. Check ingredients and labels for allergens and follow safe food-handling practices.
  • The app is provided "as is." Features may change, and we can't guarantee it will always be available or error-free.

Changes and contact
